The Problem

Why Greetly?

Traditional attendance systems are broken. They cause bottlenecks, rely on easily lost plastic cards, and are vulnerable to cheating. It's time for an upgrade.

End the Bottleneck

Long queues every morning? Greetly's 1.5s verification speed means 40 students can walk through the door seamlessly every minute.

Zero Plastic Waste

Save thousands of Ringgit on printing replacement ID cards. Your face is your permanent, unlosable identification.

Stop Time Theft

Eradicate 'buddy-punching' instantly. Our TrueDepth anti-spoofing technology ensures the person physically standing there is the real deal.

The Evolution

A Better Way Forward

From manual paper logs to AI-driven facial recognition. See why we built Greetly.

Generation 1

Paper Logbooks

Manual signing. Extremely slow, prone to errors, easy to forge, and wastes hundreds of pages of paper annually.

Generation 2

Punch Cards

Faster than writing, but requires physical cards that get lost. 'Buddy-punching' (tolong punch kad kawan) becomes a massive issue.

Generation 3

RFID / ID Cards

Digital tracking, but still relies on physical objects. Students forget them at home, leading to administrative headaches.

Generation 4

Fingerprint & QR

Eliminated physical cards, but physical touch is unhygienic (post-COVID). QR codes require students to pull out phones, causing delays.

The Ultimate Solution

Greetly 3D Facial Recognition

Zero touch. Zero wait time. Impossible to spoof. Your face is your permanent, secure key. Welcome to the future of smart attendance.

Seamless Integration

How It Works

Deploying Greetly is as easy as plugging it in. Here is the daily lifecycle.

1

One-Time Reg

Admin scans the student's face once during enrollment. Secure facial embeddings are generated and stored.

2

Walk & Scan

Every morning, the student just walks past the device. A green tick and buzzer confirm their attendance.

3

Live Sync

Data is beamed instantly via WiFi to the cloud. Parents and teachers see the update in less than a second.

Under the Hood

Powered by Edge AI

  • Brain: Raspberry Pi 4 (Quad-Core)
  • Vision: 5MP High-Def Camera
  • Feedback: OLED Display & Buzzer
  • Logic: Local Edge Computing

Unlike other systems that send massive video files to the cloud (which causes lag and privacy issues), Greetly processes the AI math directly on the device itself. It only sends secure encrypted text to the dashboard.
